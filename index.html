ABOUT / STORY

Our Story

All we ever wanted was to build a space that loved running as much as we did. A place for race day nerves, 6am long runs, and the everyday pursuit of personal bests.

01 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

We're all about the run.

Paceline was born in London out of a simple frustration: finding authentic, race-grade technical running gear meant wading through noise and lifestyle fashion. We set out to build a hub dedicated purely to runners who care about every second.

Every shoe model, race vest, and recovery tool in our stock is field-tested over hundreds of miles by our team before hitting the floor.

— Rae Sinclair & Jamie Roy, Co-Founders
02 / HOW WE OPERATE

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

Curated, not sent to us

We select product lines because they perform at the highest standard, never based on brand sponsorships or supplier pressure.

02

Community over transaction

From our weekly community 5Ks to marathon prep workshops, PACELINE is built on real connections with runners.

03

Craft in every detail

Foam energy return, seam construction, weight, and breathability — we sweat every detail so you don't have to.
