OUR STORY

MORE THAN A STORE. A RUNNING CULTURE.

Founded in 2015 by two marathon veterans, PACELINE was born out of a desire for authentic, runner-centric gear, biomechanical expertise, and an inclusive local run club.

OUR CORE PILLARS

Technical Craft

Every shoe and apparel item on our shelves undergoes testing by our crew to meet strict performance metrics.

Run Club Community

We host 3 free community runs every week—open to all paces, from first-timers to 2:20 marathoners.

Custom Fitting

Our high-speed camera gait analysis ensures you leave with footwear perfectly matched to your stride biomechanics.
